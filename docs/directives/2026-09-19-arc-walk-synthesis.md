# Arc-kind design inputs — four hypothetical walks, 2026-09-19

**Written at desk cachyos-setup-2b with the operator; design inputs for the
arc-kind item's blocked design decision. Status: MODELLED, not measured —
every claim here is a registered prediction, graded when one real arc runs
under the kind with predictions registered first. The walks deliberately
span work-kinds because the begehung's round-1 finding was "the design
generalises from one work-kind" (axis A11).**

## Walk 1 — convergent diagnosis (specimen: the Marvel Rivals freeze arc)

Boot carries goal, stage, stage-exit predicate, series trend, narrowing.
QUIET branch: the stage's conduct constraints say what a blocked stage
permits (analysis of data in hand yes; new instruments no — the moratorium
as structure). FIRE branch: stage transitions, the event lane names the
runbook. Operator appears twice: ignition and the root-gated decision.
Findings: (A) the DRIVER stays operator-ignition — a decided non-goal;
(B) stages need CONDUCT CONSTRAINTS, not only exit criteria; (C) multi-arc
scheduling re-arrives as the head-rule question from a second route;
(D) false feedback into the narrowing is the sharpest residue — aim the
independent-observer mechanism (lc-229) there first.

## Walk 2 — divergent/creative (specimen: wan2gp workflow search)

The space WIDENS: the narrowing inverts into a PALETTE — candidates with
untried/tried-with-verdict dispositions; dead ends are the main asset.
(a) A stage exit cannot be a predicate; the referee is the operator's
taste — the trigger vocabulary's third arm (`none, declared why:
operator-judged`) is what makes this expressible. (b) The ground truth is
the operator's verdict sentences, which today evaporate in chat — needs a
VERDICT-CAPTURE seam: a taste judgment writes the candidate's disposition
as a byproduct of being said. (c) Series saturation means the opposite
here: rounds yielding nothing new signal stage-exit, not futility.

## Walk 3 — routine maintenance (specimen: CachyOS general maintenance)

No hypotheses, no through-line: the arc DEGENERATES. Goals + queue +
runbooks + verify serve the session; a stage machine would be ceremony.
(a) Arcs are OPTIONAL — instantiated per-arc, never a per-project
obligation; zero-arc projects run on the head-rule over the queue. (b) The
recurring half of maintenance already has its object: the LANE. Which
forces the anti-re-invention question owed at the design round: **is an
arc a long-lived lane with persisted stage state?** One read of whether
the lane machinery EXTENDS, before any third mechanism is built.

## Walk 4 — external-consequence procedure (specimen: B-Plan planning work)

Stages are TRANSCRIBED from the legal procedure, not designed. (a) Exits
are external events (a letter arrives, a council decides) — someone must
NOTICE: intake observers, the driver question in a domain where events are
mail. (b) Deadlines are REAL DATES — a world-fact, not a lazy hold: the
schema needs a dated deadline slot WITH AN OBSERVER (a date-triggered
lane), explicitly distinct from the forbidden time-word class. (c) Some
stage exits are outward and irreversible: stages carry an OUTWARD MARK
that structurally engages the carve-out gate.

## Synthesis — eight schema requirements no single walk shows

1. Stage exits take the full trigger vocabulary: predicate, verb, AND
   operator-judged.
2. Stages carry conduct constraints (allowed/forbidden act classes).
3. The narrowing's SHAPE is a per-arc declaration: eliminative /
   palette-with-dispositions / none. (This answers begehung axis A11: the
   narrowing is not universal; its form is a slot.)
4. A verdict-capture seam for operator taste judgments in subjective
   domains — taste statements are data, booked at utterance.
5. Arcs are optional instances, never a per-project obligation.
6. Design-round obligation: check arc-vs-lane unification FIRST (walk 3's
   question) — the repo may already own this mechanism.
7. External-event exits need intake observers; deadlines get a first-class
   dated slot with an observer, exempt-by-design from the time-word ban.
8. Outward stages are marked, wiring the carve-out floor into structure.

Three of the eight (4, 6, 7) are requirements walk 1 alone would have got
WRONG, not merely missed — a diagnosis-shaped schema would have hardcoded
eliminative narrowing, predicate-only exits, and no verdict capture.
