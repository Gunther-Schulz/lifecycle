# Purpose — what lifecycle is becoming

**Written 2026-09-19, distilled with the operator over one extended session
(desk cachyos-setup-2b) and corrected by them in rounds; this document is
the north-star carrier and is amended the same way it was made — statement,
mirror, correction. It deliberately describes what lifecycle SHOULD BECOME,
not what it is.**

## The goal, in one line

**Give a project a continuous mind — and keep that mind outside every head
that thinks with it, the running session's included.**

The intelligence rented session by session is already good enough; what it
lacks is continuity and independent feedback. The project itself becomes
the persistent entity — purpose, history, dead ends, taste, standards, and
the current thread all belong to the project — and a session is the project
waking up for a few hours. The model is interchangeable compute; the WORKER
is the repo.

## The contract (the statiker contract, project-sized)

The operator says WHERE. The system knows where it is, what has been tried,
what worked, what did not, what is next, and what to ask — and asks each
thing once. The operator appears at ignition (their choice, kept), at
genuine decisions, and for taste. "I should be only needed when I am really
needed."

The average session behaves like statiker: it INVESTIGATES before it
speaks — looks first, judges first, tests first — and what reaches the
operator is only what investigation genuinely cannot settle: intent,
preference, authority over the irreversible, and each novel question once.
A proposal arrives pre-collided with reality; a GO is never spent on a plan
that dies at first contact (the measured class: evidence rots between
booking and pickup — 55% of completed items amended, evidence slot 39%
(machine-computed, this carrier's own audit 2026-09-18, post-treatment on
governed repos); a
proposal built on an unread world wastes a plan cycle and an operator
round).

## Session-independence (the second half, and the deeper one)

Continuity is NOT only cross-session. **The context window is a cache; the
project's persisted working state is the truth — at every moment,
mid-session included.** Every act writes its state as a byproduct; every
decision seam re-reads instead of recalls. A session built this way stays
on track while alive (momentum cannot silently carry it off-axis, loops are
visible as visited states, ruled-out paths cannot be re-entered innocently)
AND hands off losslessly when it dies — because nothing load-bearing ever
lived only in its head.

**Acceptance criterion — the KILL TEST:** at any arbitrary moment, kill the
session and resume with a fresh one; measured loss under kill is the same
quantity as within-session drift risk. Drive it toward zero. The close-test
("closing a session should have almost nothing left to do") is this
property observed at one moment; the full form holds at every moment.

The two drift modes are one disease at opposite settings, both the window
winning over external state: AMNESIA (state absent from view — the model
competently interprets too little into the wrong act) and HYPERFOCUS (the
window over-supplied with itself — a wrong frame compounds turn over turn,
and nothing generated inside the frame can break the frame; the way out is
by construction an input from outside it). Daneel was born from the second.

## The creed — the division of labor

> **The model supplies INTERPRETATION. The environment supplies PRESENCE,
> DEMAND, and INDEPENDENT FEEDBACK. Nothing encodes "what to do next" —
> state plus goal suffice, because deriving the next act is what the
> intelligence is good at.**

- **PRESENCE** — the right SLICE of state in view at the moment it matters,
  session start and mid-session seams alike. Presence is selection, not
  loading: the record may grow unboundedly while the per-moment slice stays
  lean (this is the containment for corpus-erosion risk). Basis: the
  governance-decay measurement (a rule absent from context: violations
  0% → 30-59%, external, lane-verified not desk-opened); retrieval fails
  at MOMENTS OF APPLICATION, not at storage.
- **DEMAND** — the seam asks, and cannot be passed unanswered. Canonical
  carrier: `docs/required-slots-as-an-autonomy-lever.md` — do not ask an
  agent to decide well; require it to WRITE at the moment of deciding. The
  slot demands the STATEMENT, never the answer: a genuinely undecidable
  question passes by saying so. Presence alone measurably under-fires (the
  adherence split: rules loaded the whole session, inert in 5 of 6
  corrections); a demand converts "should engage" into "cannot proceed
  without engaging". Self-questioning is excluded by evidence (intrinsic
  self-correction degrades); the demand is the environment's,
  deterministic. (Adherence split: measured, dotfiles journal; "5 of 6"
  is that measurement's figure, one session's sample.)
- **INDEPENDENT FEEDBACK** — every verdict the work rests on comes from
  something that cannot see or influence the answer while it is produced: a
  referee, an executed check, a second information holder reading the
  artifact. Grading prose is near-worthless (judge ceiling AUROC 0.65);
  reading state works. A player loop survives harsh, delayed, even unfair
  feedback; it cannot survive FALSE feedback.

## The living record (what makes wrong beliefs safe)

The record distinguishes FACTS-OF-RECORD (what was done/observed —
append-only history) from BELIEFS (hypotheses, verdicts, ESTABLISHED
lines — always reopenable). A belief carries its id, its BASIS, and where
known its kill-condition; downstream entries cite the belief ids they rest
on; REOPEN is a first-class verb whose act flags every citer for
re-derivation. Nothing is set in stone; reopening on evidence is the
discovery process working. A wrong belief WITH a basis is self-limiting;
a basis-less belief line is the shape the record checker refuses.

## What stays the operator's, forever

Taste, scope, naming, which arc matters, the carve-outs (irreversible and
outward acts), and each genuinely novel question — once, after which it
enters the question pipeline and never needs asking twice. This half is
the operator DESIGNING the system, not a gap. Roughly half of historical
capture events were this kind; not one was a defect (measured: 19 YES /
19 NO at event grain, capture-routes audit 2026-09-18 — one lane's range,
machine-computed).

## The felt acceptance test

The operator can walk away for a week; nothing degrades and nothing waits
stupidly; they return and one glance re-enters them; every ask queued for
them is one only they could answer; reading a transcript feels like
watching a competent colleague work, not like supervising. The day the
operator's irritation is rare enough to be notable, the goal is met.

## What would kill it (named, so the goal is falsifiable)

1. Records written, read — and the operator still needed at the same rate:
   the bottleneck was judgment and direction, not knowledge. Watched by
   the derivable-fraction metric (lc-228) and decision-absorption (lc-161).
2. Erosion: accumulated machinery degrading the capability it serves
   (external counter-evidence exists; lc-222 is the probe and the gate).
3. The instruments themselves defect-bearing — contained only by
   independence and reach discipline, never by trust in any single check.
