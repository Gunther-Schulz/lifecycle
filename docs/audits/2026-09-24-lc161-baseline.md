requires (judge rulings 2 and 3 gate R3 and R7 on it). It holds aggregates

## How far to trust it

- **Desk grading of the classifier:** 20 random classified rows were read
  in full (seed 7). 15 are clearly right and 4 borderline. 1 is wrong: a
  pasted desk message was filed as RATIFICATION where it is RELAY, but it is
  a target either way. Only one borderline row (#9, RATIFICATION vs
  DECISION) crosses the target/legitimate line. Read the 58.6% as roughly
  ±5-10 points, and the per-class figures as noisier.
- **One sample, one classifier, one seed.** The shares are estimates with
  sampling error (n = 428). No inter-rater run exists.
- **The operator often quotes the assistant back** before a `-->` reply. The
  classifier graded the post-arrow segment, which is noted per row in the
  data.
- **R1 is graded on ALREADY-IN-RECORD, which is tiny (7 of 428).** A "rise"
  after R1 ships will be hard to distinguish from noise at this sample size.
  The after arm should sample that class larger, or R1's falsifier stays
  COULD NOT VERIFY. **R3 is graded on STEER**, the second-largest target
  class, where a movement is readable.
- **Attribution across decisions is weak by construction**, since several
  ship in one window (design round §3).
- **Pre-registration of this baseline's own use:** the refocus as a whole is
  graded on targets per 100 turns, and each decision on its named class.
  **A drop smaller than the sampling error is COULD NOT VERIFY, never a
  success.**
